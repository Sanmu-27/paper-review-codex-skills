# Final Pass Checklist

## P0 Final-Upload Risks

- Accepted paper misses a promised rebuttal change.
- PDF and source zip are different versions.
- Checklist contradicts paper or is incomplete.
- Bibliography or figures fail to build in packaged source.
- De-anonymization is inconsistent with venue stage.
- Supplement contains wrong paper title, old author list, or stale results.

## P1 Trust Risks

- Abstract claims are broader than experiments.
- Contributions overlap or use vague novelty language.
- Figure captions describe old method names.
- Main text and appendix use different notation.
- Result deltas are rounded inconsistently.
- Limitation section omits an issue emphasized in reviews.

## P2 Polish

- Long captions can be shortened without losing meaning.
- Repeated caveats can be consolidated.
- Citation capitalization needs braces.
- Hyperlinks, references, and appendix labels need cleanup.
- Table footnotes need consistent abbreviations.

## Wording Patterns

Prefer:

- "In this setting, ..."
- "Across the evaluated benchmarks, ..."
- "These results suggest ..."
- "The artifact includes scripts for ..."
- "A limitation is ..."

Avoid:

- "solves"
- "guarantees" without a theorem
- "fully reproducible" without exact scope
- "all benchmarks" when only a subset was tested
- "negligible overhead" without units
