# Rebuttal Strategy Prompt

Help plan and draft a rebuttal for these paper reviews.

Use the reviews, scores, confidence, paper, appendix, and any new evidence to produce:

1. decision diagnosis
2. reviewer-by-reviewer belief map
3. issue triage: must-answer, worth-answering, defer, do-not-fight
4. evidence/action plan
5. concise rebuttal outline
6. polished rebuttal draft
7. risks and phrases to avoid

Be respectful and precise. Correct misunderstandings with anchors. Concede valid weaknesses. Do not invent experiments or claim results that have not been run.
