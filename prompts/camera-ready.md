# Camera-Ready Prompt

Audit this final or near-final research paper for camera-ready submission.

Use the final PDF/source, reviews, rebuttal, appendix, checklist, bibliography, supplement, and artifact links when available. Check:

- promised rebuttal changes
- title/abstract/intro/conclusion consistency
- contribution and limitation wording
- notation and method-name consistency
- figure/table captions, labels, units, and references
- bibliography casing and citation hygiene
- checklist consistency
- PDF/source zip/supplement version drift
- final upload risks

Output final-readiness verdict, promised-change checklist, high-risk issues, wording improvements, package issues, and exact P0/P1/P2 final actions.
